# Decision models: confidence calibration and the deferral trade-off (common core, calibration split)

Generated by `paper/pilot/confidence_calibration.py`. ECE = expected calibration error of the reported confidence against outcome correctness (10 equal-width bins); Brier = mean squared error of the confidence as a probability of a correct outcome. Deferral: the share of factual decisions with the lowest confidence is sent to a human; the unjustified rate is measured among the rest (wrong outcome or basis failure, registered key; reviewed key in the second block), and 'caught' is the share of all unjustified decisions among the deferred ones. The confidence field is what each model reports as its confidence; Basal's equals the probability of the chosen action, Liquid d1's and Luna's are below it, so an ECE on the probability of the chosen action is given as well.
